# Get started with Stovo

This guide introduces the web workspace after your organization has deployed Stovo. You need the workspace address and an account or invitation from its administrator. The first owner is created during initial setup; later registration is invite-based. Interface labels below are in English and can differ with the selected language or deployment.

## Join and sign in

Open the address supplied by your administrator. If you have an invitation, open its link and follow the registration prompts. If it is expired or cannot be used, ask the person who invited you for another. Sign in to the workspace using the available sign-in method for your account.

## Start a conversation

1. Open **Chats** in the navigation and select an existing conversation, or select **Start a chat** (the plus button).
2. Search for a member and select them for a direct conversation. To create a group, choose **New group**, enter a name, select members and choose **Create**.
3. Enter a message in the composer and send it. Use message actions to reply or react; use **Search messages or members** to find people or earlier messages.

Channels and voice channels can also be created from **New chat** when your role permits them. Chat creation, messaging and invitations can be restricted by your workspace or member privacy settings.

## Share and find files

Attach a file from the message composer to share it in a conversation. Open **Files** in the main navigation to search and filter files, browse shared content, or use **Upload file**. File and collection access depends on the permissions granted to you; ask the chat or workspace administrator if you cannot open something you expect to see.

## Meet and make plans

Use the audio or video call action in a conversation to start a call. Your browser may ask for microphone or camera access. Guest meetings can be created from **New chat → Plan guest meeting** when available. Open **Plans** in the main navigation to view shared plans and schedule details.

Calls and optional transcription depend on the services and features enabled by the operator. If a call does not connect, check device permissions and contact your workspace administrator. Operators can consult the [Compose network guidance](../self-hosting/docker-compose.md#calls-and-public-access).

## Change your preferences or get help

Open **Settings** to review your profile, notifications and privacy preferences. For invitations, missing access or unavailable features, contact your workspace administrator. For more detail, see [Conversations and calls](conversations-and-calls.md) and [Files and plans](files-and-plans.md). For software defects, use the [issue templates](../../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/); do not include private messages, credentials or personal data.
