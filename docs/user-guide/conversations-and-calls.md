# Conversations and calls

These instructions describe the web client. The controls and chat types available to you depend on permissions, privacy settings and the services enabled on your workspace. For joining a workspace, start with [Get started with Stovo](getting-started.md).

## Choose or create a chat

Open **Chats** and select a conversation from the sidebar. To find a member or message, use **Search messages or members**. Select **Start a chat** (plus) to search for a person or choose **New group**, **New channel**, or **New voice channel** when those options are available.

For a group, enter a name, select members and choose **Create**. A channel is intended for broadcast posts; a voice channel provides a place for audio participation. Direct messages and creation of particular chat types may be restricted by workspace policy or another member's privacy settings.

## Send and revisit messages

Select a chat, enter your message in the composer and send it. Use the message actions to **Reply** in context or add a reaction. You can share an attachment from the composer and find shared content later under **Files** or in the chat's shared content. Scheduled messages and drafts are accessible from the sidebar's drafts and scheduled area.

If you cannot find a message, check that you selected the right workspace and chat, then search for distinctive words. Your access to messages and files depends on your membership and permissions.

## Start or join a call

In a conversation, choose the **Audio call** or **Video call** action. Grant browser microphone or camera permission when requested and review your device selection in call settings. Available call controls include microphone, camera and screen sharing; device and browser capabilities affect which controls work.

To invite an external guest, use **New chat → Plan guest meeting** when the option is enabled. Call availability depends on the instance's media and network services; transcription is optional and needs additional configuration. If a call cannot connect, check device permissions and ask your administrator to review the [call network configuration](../self-hosting/docker-compose.md#calls-and-public-access).
