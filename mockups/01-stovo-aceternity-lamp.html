Frequently Asked Questions

Is Stovo open source?

Stovo is published under a transparent Source-Available license. Code is public for auditing, testing, and self-hosting with full inspection rights.

What is the status of the Company Brain feature?

Company Brain is currently in closed Early Alpha testing. It correlates communications, documentation, and task threads across the organization. It is not yet enabled on public servers.

Can I move from Stovo Cloud to Self-Hosted later?

Absolutely. You can generate a single cryptographic export archive containing all messages, threads, users, and object files and restore it into your own Docker cluster in under 10 minutes.
